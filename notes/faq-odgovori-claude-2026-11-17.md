## Šta tim treba da potvrdi
1. **„Can you work with our developers?“** Na sajtu nigde ne piše da radite zajedno sa developerima klijenta. „Yes“ je moja pretpostavka iz same postavke pitanja. Ostatak odgovora (dokumentovan build, snimljena primopredaja, isti ljudi od prvog poziva) stoji na sajtu.
2. **Posle lansiranja, nedelja ili 30 dana:** tabela na Services kaže „30 days of support“, a koraci na servisnim stranicama i Pricing kažu „a week on call“. Odgovori prate svoju stranicu, pa se razlikuju. Treba izabrati jedno i ispraviti sajt.
3. **Trajanje se ne slaže između stranica:** Services kaže Design & Development 6 do 8 nedelja i Webflow Development 4 do 6, a Pricing kartice kažu 3 do 5 i 6 do 8. Zato odgovor na Pricing ne navodi brojeve nego upućuje na kartice.
4. **Webflow Development stranica** prikazuje iste korake kao Design & Development (uključujući „Brand and copy“). Moji odgovori za tu stranicu prate opis usluge sa Services (vaš dizajn, copy dolazi sa njim, 4 do 6 nedelja), ne te korake.

## Services (/services)
- **Who writes the copy?** We do on Design & Development, SEO & AEO and Growth, where strategy and copy are part of the work. On Webflow Development you bring the design and its copy, and we build it in Webflow.
- **How long does it actually take?** Design & Development takes 6 to 8 weeks from kickoff to launch, and Webflow Development 4 to 6 weeks from Figma to live. SEO & AEO brings the first wins in 6 weeks, and Growth runs month to month.
- **What does it cost?** Design & Development and Webflow Development are one fixed price. SEO & AEO and Growth are monthly. You get a plan and a fixed number after the first call, and if the scope moves, you hear what it costs before anything is added.
- **What happens after launch?** Design & Development and Webflow Development include 30 days of support after launch. SEO & AEO and Growth keep going month to month, with a monthly report.
- **Can you work with our developers?** Yes. Every build is documented and handed over on a recording, so your developers can pick it up at any point. The people on the first call are the people who ship your site.

## Design & Development (/services/design-development)
- **Who writes the copy?** We do. Messaging and full copy for every page are part of the project, written with you in weeks 1 to 2, next to the art direction.
- **How long does it actually take?** 6 to 8 weeks from kickoff to launch: discovery in week 1, brand and copy in weeks 1 to 2, design in weeks 2 to 4, the Webflow build in weeks 4 to 6, then QA and launch in weeks 6 to 8.
- **What does it cost?** One fixed price, agreed after the first call. We do not track hours, and if the scope moves, you hear what it costs the same week, before anything is added.
- **What happens after launch?** We take the site live and stay on call for the first week. You get a recorded handover, so your team can update the site without us, and Growth can start the week after launch if you want us to keep going.
- **Can you work with our developers?** Yes. The site is built on a clean class system and CMS, and the recorded handover walks your team through it, so your developers can pick it up at any point.

## Webflow Development (/services/webflow-development)
- **Who writes the copy?** You do. Webflow Development builds the design you already have, so the copy comes with it. If you need the copy written, that is part of Design & Development.
- **How long does it actually take?** 4 to 6 weeks from Figma to live, on a launch date we commit to after the first call.
- **What does it cost?** One fixed price, based on your designs and agreed after the first call. We do not track hours, and if the scope moves, you hear what it costs before anything is added.
- **What happens after launch?** We take it live and stay on call for the first week, and you get a recorded handover so your team can run the CMS from day one. Growth can start the week after launch if you want us to keep going.
- **Can you work with our developers?** Yes. We build from your Figma files, and the build is documented and handed over on a recording, so your developers can work on it after launch.

## SEO & AEO (/services/seo-aeo)
- **Who writes the copy?** We do. Strategy and copy are included: pages and articles are written every month, with clear answers built to rank and get quoted by AI.
- **How long does it actually take?** The audit takes weeks 1 to 2, research week 2 and the structure work weeks 3 to 4. After that, content ships every month, and the first wins show in about 6 weeks.
- **What does it cost?** SEO & AEO is a monthly service. You get a plan and the number after the first call, and if the scope moves, you hear what it costs before anything is added.
- **What happens after launch?** There is no hand-off moment. We track rankings and AI citations every month, send a monthly report and double down on what works.
- **Can you work with our developers?** Yes. We work in your existing Webflow site, on schema, metadata, internal linking and the CMS setup, so your developers can keep building as usual.

## Growth (/services/growth)
- **Who writes the copy?** We do. New pages, sections and campaigns are designed and written when you need them, as part of the monthly team time.
- **How long does it actually take?** The audit takes weeks 1 to 2 and the quarterly roadmap is ready in week 2. From then on, changes are built, checked and live in Webflow every week.
- **What does it cost?** Growth is monthly, month to month, with no long contracts and hours that never expire. You get the number after the first call.
- **What happens after launch?** Growth is what happens after launch: weekly releases, A/B tests and a monthly report that feeds back into the plan.
- **Can you work with our developers?** Yes. We share one roadmap and one channel with your team, and every change goes through QA before it is live.
